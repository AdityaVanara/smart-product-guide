A story from our shoppers

Imagine a student with a budget of ₹25,000 searching for a new phone. On one app the price is lower, on another the reviews look better, and a third shows a seller she has never heard of. Twelve tabs later she is still unsure, so she closes the app and decides to buy it later.

This is not a rare case. In our survey of 57 shoppers, 68.4% said confusion makes them delay or cancel a purchase at least sometimes, and 54.4% doubt product quality. When we asked what would help most, 35.1% chose more reliable customer reviews.

That is why we put prices, ratings, specs and seller details side by side in one place. But the story does not end here. Your feedback is the next chapter: tell us what worked, what confused you and what you would change.
