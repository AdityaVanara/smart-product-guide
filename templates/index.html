Our Design Thinking process

Each stage of the process is a real part of this website, not only a slide.

1. Empathize

We surveyed shoppers and listened to their frustrations.

2. Define

We turned the answers into one clear problem statement (below) and a list of customer needs.

3. Ideate

We listed ideas: side-by-side comparison, budget filter, seller score, adjusted ratings, simple explanations.

4. Prototype

We built the Smart Product Comparison Guide: products, comparison and AI picks.

5. Test

Users rate the guide on the Feedback page.

Problem statement

Online shoppers who compare products on Amazon, Flipkart and Meesho need a simple way to see prices, ratings, specifications and seller reliability together, because scattered and conflicting information makes them doubt quality, delay purchases or give up.

Customer needs

  • Reviews they can trust
  • Specifications compared in one table
  • The lowest price across platforms
  • Knowing if the seller is reliable
  • A recommendation that explains itself

Idea map

Imagine a student with a budget of ₹25,000 searching for a new phone. On one app the price is lower, on another the reviews look better, and a third shows a seller she has never heard of. Twelve tabs later she is still unsure, so she closes the app. In our survey, 68.4% said confusion makes them delay or cancel a purchase at least sometimes. That is why we put everything side by side in one place.
